Before you sign up — what this is
  • A personal project on a free hosting tier, not a service with an SLA.
  • What gets stored: whatever you enter or upload — your name, contact details, employment history and any résumé file.
  • Not encrypted at rest. The operator can read the database.
  • AI features run on the operator's API key, so your account is capped at ${{ "%.2f"|format(budget) }} of AI spend a month. The résumé checker, JD scoring, the quality gate and the tracker cost nothing and always work.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, from Settings. That removes it, not just hides it.
  • No password reset — there is no mail server. Losing the password means losing the account.
The honest summary: fine for trying the tool on real work, wrong for your only copy of anything.
